# Architecture and boundaries

## What Wildline means by continuity

**Memory** retains a record. **Continuity** preserves enough of the record's source, interpretation, revision and permissions for a later interaction to begin from a supported understanding without requiring the user to reconstruct the path.

This is a design goal. Some mechanisms have been verified in bounded Lab and judge scenarios; the full portable product is not finished.

The broader **relational continuity** thesis asks whether carrying forward shared challenges, disagreement, corrections and discoveries can make a continuing human–AI working relationship more useful. **The Reachable Unknown** names the resulting research question about useful possibilities. That advantage remains unmeasured and independently unproven; this reader's recovery mechanism does not demonstrate it. See [Roadmap and vision](ROADMAP-AND-VISION.md).

```text
Authorized source (Bee, user, or another permitted source)
                     |
                     v
             Evidence + provenance
                     |
                     v
       Interpretations and uncertainty
                     |
                     v
       Corrections / revisions / time
                     |
                     v
     User authority and policy boundaries
                     |
                     v
     Recoverable context for a fresh process
```

The arrows represent intended processing responsibilities, not a claim that every stage is currently live-connected in the distributable public source package.

## Five questions

1. **What happened?** Preserve source data and when it was observed.
2. **What changed?** Record corrections, revisions, withdrawal and supersession without silently rewriting history.
3. **What is uncertain?** Preserve unresolved alternatives and incomplete observations.
4. **What has authority?** A captured statement is not permission to act, change policy, or define the user.
5. **What carries forward?** Recover current, supported and authorized context in a later process or session.

## Three operations: 411, 413, 412

- **411 Recover:** Retrieve relevant history, corrections and uncertainty without pretending recall is infallible.
- **413 Challenge:** Test the favored interpretation, seek contradictions and preserve meaningful dissent.
- **412 Reconstruct:** Carry forward what survives challenge while leaving current conclusions revisable.

The sequence describes a **human-AI reasoning and governance method** observed in Lyra Core. The public H5 reader does not implement the complete three-operator research system. Reconstructions are external context revisions, not retraining the underlying foundation model.

## Authority and governance

- Current explicit user direction and actual source evidence outrank stale interpretations.
- Source evidence, inferred meaning and permission to act are separate.
- Uncertain or contested interpretations may remain unresolved.
- A Lab PASS does not authorize production promotion.
- Human approval is required for consequential state or authority changes, according to the relevant governance boundary.
- This documentation asserts no fresh governance status and approves no pending item.

## Important exclusions

The existing project does **not** demonstrate universal live synchronization, self-directed external action, automatic device-origin attribution, a generally deployed care workflow, independent new-user adaptation, foundation-model retraining or verified AI consciousness. Any of those would require separate proof and governance.

**Portable constitution. Non-portable biography.**

## VERIFIED / EXPERIMENTAL / FUTURE VISION

VERIFIED means a bounded behavior supported by an attributed retained report or local operation check. EXPERIMENTAL identifies separate Lab work. FUTURE VISION identifies capabilities not delivered here. None means production readiness or independent physical-machine verification.

## Actual target flow

Fictional ordered A/B pair → acquisition child exits → separate H5 reader verifies local seal → actual recovered comparison. External ingestion is disabled.

Small reader packages use standard-library Python and local H5 code, not a language model or SQLite Core. Broader Reflection/Between are separate EXPERIMENTAL Lab work, absent from those packages. A complete new-user product is FUTURE VISION, not a shipped feature.
