# Wildline questions and answers

## Is Wildline a chatbot?

No. Wildline is being developed as a **continuity architecture** that can support AI applications. It is not intended to replace the underlying language model, Amazon Bee, or an existing assistant.

## Didn't ChatGPT already have memory?

The founder's problem wasn't just remembering past statements. It was preserving **how understanding changed**, why something was revised and which interpretation still deserved to influence the next interaction.

## What is relational continuity?

It means carrying forward the consequences of sustained human–AI collaboration: shared challenges, disagreement, corrections and discovery. The aim is a more useful thinking partnership whose understanding remains open to challenge, not automatic agreement, human imitation, or a claim that AI has human feelings. The public reader demonstrates a small recovery mechanism, not this complete relationship.

## What is The Reachable Unknown?

It is the research hypothesis that sustained, accountable collaboration could help a person discover useful possibilities they would be less likely to reach through disconnected AI interactions. The founder's collaboration experience motivates the question; its advantage has not been measured or independently established. Previous synthetic experiments are preliminary mechanism investigations, not proof of superior discovery or emergent intelligence. See [Roadmap and research](ROADMAP-AND-VISION.md).

## What is the difference between Lyra Core and Wildline?

**Lyra Core** is the private, bespoke working reference experience. **Wildline** is the effort to extract the underlying mechanisms for others. A new user should be able to create their own continuity without importing the founder's history.

## What does Bee do here?

Bee captures real-world context and exposes account data. Wildline treats those outputs as **source evidence**, not automatic truth or authority to act.

## Can I run the public-source demonstration without Bee?

The public Continuity Reader is deliberately offline and uses **fictional fixtures**. It does not include the founder's live Bee adapter or personal evidence. It is not the complete private Bee-track entry.

## Have you demonstrated a real Bee connection?

Yes, within a bounded scope. A founder-authorized v1.1 run performed one authenticated Bee Fact read and recovered its selected information in a distinct process. Its receipt was independently checked. That is not proof of automatic synchronization or live corrections.

## Can the system detect changes automatically?

The public and historical demonstrations include specific, bounded interpretations of recorded observations and lifecycle changes. The single live v1.1 observation doesn't establish a change or no-change conclusion by itself. A general-purpose live synchronization system is not currently claimed.

## Does Wildline decide who someone is?

The design explicitly rejects that outcome. The person should be able to challenge assumptions and control which information has authority. **Continuity Without Captivity** means historical context is useful but not final.

## Is the AI being retrained or made conscious?

The foundation model is not retrained by this continuity mechanism. Wildline preserves external, revisable working context. None of the demonstrated results establishes consciousness, personhood or independent agency.

## Is it ready for patients, staff or incarcerated hospice care?

No. Peer support, hospice, caregiving and living-legacy applications are possibilities to research with substantial consent, privacy, clinical/professional and institutional oversight. The current prototype isn't a deployed care product.

## Why open-source a small reader?

It is a privacy-conscious demonstration of a reusable mechanism. The founder's personal Core and sensitive Bee data should not become part of someone else's downloaded system. ## What is the role of 411, 413 and 412?

These name a development/reasoning rhythm: **Recover**, **Challenge**, **Reconstruct**. The small offline reader doesn't claim to implement every capability explored in that method.
