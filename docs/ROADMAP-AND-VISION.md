# Roadmap and vision

## Where we began

The founder needed to find commitments and decisions buried in a constant flow of workplace information. Bee captured context, the Daily Command Center made it useful, and Lyra Core made some of the collaboration's accumulated changes recoverable.

**Wildline exists to make the reusable capability portable, without transferring the founder's biography.**

## FUTURE VISION — The Reachable Unknown

**Can sustained, accountable human–AI collaboration help a person discover useful possibilities they would be less likely to reach through disconnected AI interactions?**

This is Wildline's central research question. The founder has experienced the value of continuing collaboration, but we have not measured its advantage over ordinary AI interactions or independently established superior discovery.

Relational continuity would preserve the consequences of working together: failed ideas, challenges, disagreements, corrections, unexpected connections and discoveries. An evolving working relationship could revisit why an interpretation was rejected, compare competing interpretations, and use productive disagreement to make a question or possibility reachable that neither participant recognized at the beginning.

This is a hypothesis about a useful thinking partnership. It is not a goal of automatic agreement, human imitation, or a claim that an AI possesses human feelings. A person's history should inform what happens next without becoming permanent authority over their future.

**411 Recover → 413 Challenge → 412 Reconstruct** names the method: recover relevant history and uncertainty; challenge the favored interpretation; reconstruct what survives while keeping conclusions revisable.

**Memory carries information. Continuity carries consequences. Relationship gives those consequences meaning. And collaboration may open the door to the Reachable Unknown.**

## VERIFIED — the foundation, not the research advantage

The published public Continuity Reader demonstrates bounded fresh-process recovery with fictional evidence, source provenance, uncertainty and authority NONE. It is a rule-based offline component, not the complete Wildline system or a relational AI product.

Separately, the private Bee Bridge v1.1 verified one explicitly authorized genuine Bee acquisition followed by recovery in another process. One observation establishes neither change nor no-change, synchronization, automatic learning or production readiness. Its private source content, adapter and receipts are absent from this repository.

Neither result demonstrates the broader relational advantage. Historical correction/withdrawal replay is a separate preserved demonstration, not a new live change or a feature included in this public reader.

## EXPERIMENTAL — preliminary mechanism investigations

Previous synthetic Reachable Unknown experiments explored possible mechanisms in bounded Lab work. They are **preliminary mechanism investigations, not independent proof of superior discovery or emergent intelligence**. They are not distributed in this reader.

The future research task is to evaluate useful discovery against disconnected AI interactions and simpler memory systems, with clear comparison criteria. Personal experience and synthetic demonstrations motivate that question; they do not answer it.

## FUTURE VISION — product challenge: a new person's empty continuity core

The first complete portable Wildline experience should show: independent setup; authorized source ingestion; user-owned correction; visible uncertainty; fresh-session context recovery; and bounded recovery after failure. A teacher, leader or caregiver should develop their own working history rather than inherit the founder's preferences or identity.

This experience remains a goal. The public Continuity Reader, including this RC3-docs.1 editorial revision, is not that whole product.

## FUTURE VISION — research directions, not shipped features

### Wonder and governed challenge

We want an AI that recognizes patterns without deciding they are permanent. The 411/413/412 rhythm asks the system and user to recover, challenge and reconstruct an understanding rather than merely reinforce familiarity.

### The In-Between

The In-Between is a related future exploration of governed working context between interactions. It connects to the Reachable Unknown question above without establishing a discovery advantage. The separate experimental Between composition and the public reader remain bounded mechanisms, not proven autonomous intelligence.

### Recognizable change, without a fixed identity

The original narrative draws inspiration from the Lorenz attractor and fractal geometry: a recognizable pattern may persist through changing trajectories. This is an **analogy**, not a claim that Wildline implements those mathematical systems or that human-AI collaboration follows proven fractal dynamics. It motivates a falsifiable design question: how can continuity remain recognizable without limiting future possibility?

### Person-governed living legacy

One possibility is continuity that helps a person revisit changing meaning across a lifetime and choose what to preserve. It must never pretend to replace them, record everything by default, or decide what their life meant.

### Caregiving and peer hospice support

A particularly meaningful long-term possibility is continuity to support caregivers, including peer hospice support involving incarcerated individuals. Any such application would need stringent privacy, consent, dignity, clinical/professional oversight, and institutional governance. There is **no deployed medical or correctional care product here**.

## The open question

**What am I missing? → What changed? → What else is possible?**

The roadmap should preserve that openness without confusing speculation with permission or verified evidence.

**Continuity Without Captivity.**
